What it costs

Most metal costs nothing to leave.

Before you come

Five minutes at home, in and out at the gate.

  • Metal in one pile, everything else in another
  • Gas bottles off BBQs and heaters
  • Fuel and oil drained from mowers, engines and machinery
  • Mats off trampolines and mattresses off bed frames
  • Car battery terminals taped
  • Enclosed shoes and gloves for unloading
  • Kids and pets stay in the vehicle

The rest of your load

Got rubbish in there too?

Bring it in the same trip. {{FACILITY}} is a transfer station, so anything that isn't metal goes through at its usual rate. Sort the metal out first and it stays no charge.
