Services

Collections for a tonne or more.

Three ways to move scrap metal off your site, each with one service fee quoted before we come and a weighbridge docket for every load. Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria, and further afield by arrangement.

01

One-off collections

Strip-outs, refits, clean-ups and site clears. We quote the job, bring the crew and truck, and take it in one go.

Suits
Office strip-outs, warehouse refits, demolition, farm clean-ups and depot clears.
How it works
Tell us what's there and where it sits. We quote, book a day that suits, load it and weigh it.
Good to know
Tell us about stairs, lifts, access times and inductions when you ask for a quote.

02

Bins on site

For steady volumes from workshops, factories and building sites. A bin on site, swapped when it's full.

Suits
Fabricators, workshops, manufacturers and building sites with offcuts every week.
How it works
We place a bin where it suits your site. Call when it's full, or set a regular swap.
Good to know
We confirm the bin size and where it can sit when we quote.

03

Regular collections

Scheduled pick-ups for depots, multi-site businesses and councils, on a timetable that suits your operation.

Suits
Council depots, utilities, logistics yards and businesses with more than one site.
How it works
We agree a timetable and the sites. Each load is weighed and docketed.
Good to know
Environmental reporting runs through YARTA, so your records stay in one place.

Before we come

A few things that keep the day simple.

Tell us about these when you ask for a quote, and the crew arrives with the right gear and the right plan.

  • Site rules, inductions and access times
  • Where it sits: dock, yard, upper floors, stairs or lifts
  • Drives and SSDs removed or wiped from servers and computers
  • Batteries out of equipment. They travel separately, and we take them too
  • Fuel, oil and coolant drained from engines and machinery
  • Anything we can't take kept apart: asbestos, chemicals, full or hired gas cylinders

Where we collect

Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria.
