Which one is you?

Under a tonne, drop it off. A tonne or more, we collect.

A tonne is 1,000 kg. That's more than any trailer without brakes is allowed to weigh fully loaded (750 kg), or about twenty empty four-drawer steel filing cabinets.

A tonne or more? Leave it to us.

Strip-outs, clean-ups and steady volumes from businesses, sites and depots. One service fee quoted before we come, our crew and truck, and a weighbridge docket for every load.

Offices and fit-outs

Filing cabinets, lockers, compactus, shelving, desk frames and server racks from strip-outs and moves.

A bin on site for offcuts, swapped when it's full. Machinery, motors, cable and old plant.

Warehouses and sites

Pallet racking, roll cages and stillages. Building, demolition and council depot steel.
