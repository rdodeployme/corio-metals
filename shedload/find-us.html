Do you pay for scrap metal?

Nope. We don't buy scrap, and clean metal costs nothing to drop off. It's the easy way to clear the shed, and it all goes back into making new metal.

Drop off or collection?

Under a tonne, drop it off. A tonne or more, we'll collect. The load scale shows you where you sit.

Do I need to book a drop-off?

Not for a car, ute or trailer. We're open Monday to Saturday; call Nigel for gate times.

Anything extra at the gate?

A few things carry a handling fee: gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims. See the gate fees.

What does a collection cost?

One service fee, quoted before we come. It depends on what it is, how much, the work on site and the distance. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Where do you collect from?

Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria, and further afield by arrangement.

Hard drives and batteries?

Remove or wipe drives and SSDs before we collect servers or computers. Batteries come out of equipment and travel separately, and we take them too.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, and anything still holding fuel or oil. See the list.
