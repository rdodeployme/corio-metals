How it works

From the call to the docket.

Nothing is booked until you approve the quote. You deal with Nigel the whole way through.

  1. 01

    Tell us

    What it is, roughly how much, where it sits on site and when suits. Photos help.

  2. 02

    Get a quote

    We confirm what we can take and quote one service fee.

  3. 03

    We collect

    A bin on site or a load on the day. Our crew follows your site rules and inductions.

  4. 04

    Weighed and reported

    Every load crosses our weighbridge. The docket comes to you, with reporting through YARTA.

Who calls us

Big clean-ups, steady volumes.

  • Offices and fit-outs. Filing cabinets, lockers, shelving and server racks, with drives removed or wiped first.
  • Warehouses. Pallet racking, roll cages and stillages from moves and refits.
  • Builders and demolition. Offcuts, reo, roofing iron, fencing and frames.
  • Workshops and factories. A bin on site for offcuts, swapped when it's full.
  • Farms. Old fencing, star pickets, machinery and dismantled sheds.
  • Councils and government. Depot clean-ups with a docket for every load.

The service fee

One fee, quoted first.

We don't buy scrap metal. You get one service fee for the job, and it depends on:

  • What it is, how much, and how it's sorted
  • The work on site: access, stairs and any dismantling
  • A one-off load, a bin on site, or regular pick-ups
  • How far the site is from North Geelong

Environmental reporting through YARTA Environmental Sustainability, a Recycle Group initiative.
