Crux Metals/What we collect

What we collect

If it's mostly metal, we'll take it.

Equipment and furniture that is mostly steel, stainless, copper, aluminium or brass, working or not. Items marked Specialist need extra handling, which shows in the service fee. If yours isn't on the list, ask.
