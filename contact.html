Questions

Before you head over.

Drop off or collection: which is me?

Under a tonne, drop it off at Furner Avenue. A tonne or more, we can collect it from your business or site. The load check will tell you which way to go.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

No. We don't buy scrap, and clean scrap metal costs nothing to drop off. You get rid of it the easy way, and it goes back into making new metal.

Is there a fee for anything?

A few items carry a handling fee: gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims. Anything in your load that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate. See the gate guide.

Do I need to book a drop-off?

Not for a car, ute or trailer. We're open Monday to Saturday; call for gate times. For a truck or anything over a tonne, book a collection instead.

What does a collection cost?

Every collection carries one service fee, quoted before we come. It depends on what it is, how much, the work on site, access and distance. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Where do you collect from?

Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria, and further by arrangement. Tell us where the site is when you book.

What about hard drives and data?

We don't take data-bearing drives. Remove or wipe drives and SSDs before we collect servers, computers or cabinets.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, and anything still holding fuel or oil. See the full list.
