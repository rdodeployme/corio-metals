What we collect

Every kind of scrap metal.

Ferrous and non-ferrous, from offices, warehouses, building sites, workshops, farms and depots. If there's a tonne or more of it, we can collect it.

01

Structural and building

Beams, columns, plate, angle, reo bar and mesh, roofing iron, purlins, fencing and frames.

02

Racking and storage

Pallet racking, shelving, roll cages, stillages, lockers and compactus.

03

Office and IT

Filing cabinets, desk frames, server racks and cabinets, with drives removed or wiped first.

04

Plant and machinery

Old machines, motors, pumps, gearboxes and engine blocks, drained of fuel and oil.

05

Non-ferrous

Copper pipe and cable, brass fittings, aluminium frames and extrusion, and stainless steel.

06

Whitegoods in bulk

Fridges, freezers, washers, dryers, ovens and hot water units from fit-outs and clean-outs.

07

Vehicles and parts

Rims, radiators and engine blocks. Car bodies and big machinery need a call first.

08

Batteries

Removed from equipment and kept apart, because they travel separately. We take them too.

What we can't take

Keep these apart from the metal.

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid
