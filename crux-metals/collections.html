How it works

From the first call to the docket.

You deal with Nigel the whole way through. Nothing is booked until you approve the quote.

  1. 01

    Tell us

    What it is, roughly how much, where it sits on site and when suits. Photos help.

  2. 02

    Quote

    We confirm what we can take and quote one service fee.

  3. 03

    Collect

    Our crew and trucks, a bin on site or a load on the day. We follow your site rules and inductions.

  4. 04

    Weigh and report

    Every load crosses our weighbridge. The docket comes to you, with reporting through YARTA.

Three ways to collect

One-off, on site or on a timetable.

Minimum one tonne: about twenty steel filing cabinets, or more than a trailer without brakes may weigh fully loaded (750 kg).

One-off collections

Strip-outs, refits, clean-ups and site clears. We quote the job, bring the crew and truck, and take it in one go.

Bins on site

For steady volumes from workshops, factories and building sites. A bin on site, swapped when it's full.

Regular collections

Scheduled pick-ups for depots, multi-site businesses and councils, on a timetable that suits your operation.

The service fee

One fee, quoted first.

We don't buy scrap metal. You get one service fee for the job, and it depends on:

  • What it is, how much, and how it's sorted
  • The work on site: access, stairs and any dismantling
  • A one-off load, a bin on site or regular pick-ups
  • How far the site is from North Geelong
  • Specialist items that need extra handling
