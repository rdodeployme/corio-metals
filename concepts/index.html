One offer, five versions.

Five versions of the scrap metal site for 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong. Each one has a clear public drop-off and a commercial path, and none of them pays for metal. They differ in name, look, which path leads, and the tool on the home page. Tap the big buttons at the top of any page to swap between them; you stay on the matching page.

The lead: Recycled Metals

Richard's notes, built: Recycled Metals, by the tonne. Free drop-off at any volume, four ways to move metal, the 766-item list with a drop-off column, and a map of the towns covered.

For the public first: The Metal Drop. The name says exactly what it does for signage and Google, and its item checker answers the question people ask before they load up.

Crux Metals for a new national name. Shedload for the most memorable ads and signs, after a check on Shedload Pty Ltd.

The same in all five

  • Public drop-off at Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue. Clean scrap metal is no charge: we don't pay for it, and you don't pay to leave it.
  • Handling fees on a few items only: gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims, as on Recycle North Geelong's price list.
  • A commercial path in every version: collections from one tonne in four of them, and bins and big jobs by enquiry in Recycled Metals.
  • A weighbridge docket for every collected load. YARTA reporting in four versions; Richard's notes took it out of Recycled Metals.
  • Nigel Taylor as the only contact: 0427 888 222, nigel@recycle.net.au. Enquiry forms open an email to Nigel.
  • The same can't-take list: asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, anything holding fuel or oil.
  • “A Recycle Group business” with the official Recycle mark near the logo and in every footer.
  • The same address, hours, fees, can't-take list and contact from one shared source file, so the facts can't drift between versions.
