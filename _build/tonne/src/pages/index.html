08 · Questions

The short answers.

Is drop-off really free?

Yes. Clean scrap metal is free to drop off at {{STREET}}, any volume, from a bucket to a B‑double. A few items carry a handling fee: gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims.

Is scrap metal paid for?

No. Recycled Metals doesn't buy scrap metal. Drop-off is free, and bins and collections are by enquiry.

How does a collection work?

Send an enquiry or call {{CONTACT}} on {{PHONE}} with what it is, roughly how much and where it sits. {{CONTACT}} comes back with what suits: a bin, regular pick-ups, an operator with an excavator and grapple, or a tilt tray.

What bin sizes are there?

1 to 2 m³ bins for regular steel on a regular pick-up, and skip bins in 15, 32 and 42 m³.

What can't come in?

{{CANT_SHORT}} Car bodies and big machinery: call {{CONTACT}} first.
