What we take

Mostly metal? We'll take it.

Ferrous and non-ferrous, from a boot full of offcuts to a warehouse of racking. Clean scrap metal is no charge at the gate; a few items carry a handling fee.

Metals

Every metal, every grade.

Steel or copper, rusty or bright. Keep the non-ferrous metals apart from the steel and unloading takes minutes.

Copper

Pipe, cable, wire, hot water cylinders, motor windings.

Brass

Taps, valves, fittings, plumbing brass, door hardware.

Aluminium

Window frames, sheet, extrusion, rims, cans.

Stainless

Sinks, benches, kitchen and food equipment, tanks.

Lead

Flashing, wheel weights, sheet lead.

Steel and iron

Structural steel, sheet, pipe, cast iron, machinery.

Electric motors

Motors, pumps and alternators, kept whole.

Cable

Copper and aluminium cable, insulated or stripped.

Before you load up

Save time at the gate.

Sort the metal from the rubbish

Anything that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate, so keep it separate.

Drain it

No fuel, oil or water left in mowers, machinery, engines or tanks.

Bottles off

Gas bottles come off BBQs and heaters. Empty, customer-owned bottles carry a small fee; hired ones go back to the supplier.

We can't take

Please leave these at home.

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid

Not sure? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222 before you load up.
