Do you pay for scrap metal?

No. We don't buy scrap metal. Clean scrap metal is no charge to drop off, and collections carry one service fee, quoted before we come.

Drop off or collection?

Under a tonne, drop it off at 116 Furner Avenue. A tonne or more, we collect it. About twenty steel filing cabinets make a tonne.

Do I need to book?

Not for a car, ute or trailer. We're open {{HOURS}}; call {{CONTACT}} for gate times.

Anything extra at the gate?

Gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims carry a handling fee. See the gate fees.

Hard drives and batteries?

Remove or wipe drives and SSDs before we collect servers or computers. Batteries come out of equipment and travel separately, and we take them too.
