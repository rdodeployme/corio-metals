Questions

Before you head over.

Drop off or collection: which is me?

Under a tonne, drop it off at Furner Avenue. A tonne or more, we can collect it from your business or site. The load estimate will tell you which way to go.

Do I need to book a drop-off?

Not for a car, ute or trailer. Call ahead for gate hours. For a truck or anything over a tonne, book a collection instead.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

It depends on the metal and the load. Clean, sorted copper, brass, aluminium and stainless are worth the most. Mixed steel is worth less, and some items carry a handling fee. See the price board.

Why can't you pay cash?

Victorian law has banned cash payments for scrap metal since May 2018. Any payment is by bank transfer, and we have to record your name, address and ID. Bring your licence.

What does a collection cost?

Every collection is quoted before we come. The quote depends on what it is, how much, the work on site, access and distance. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Where do you collect from?

Geelong and the surrounding region, and further across Victoria by arrangement. Tell us where the site is when you book.

What about hard drives and data?

We don't take data-bearing drives. Remove or wipe drives and SSDs before we collect servers, computers or cabinets.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, anything still holding fuel or oil, and general rubbish. See the full list.
