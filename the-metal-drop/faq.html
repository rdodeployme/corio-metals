FAQ and contact

Got a question?

Still not sure? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222 or send a message below.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

No. We don't buy scrap metal, and clean scrap metal costs nothing to drop off. It's the easy way to get rid of it, and it goes back into making new metal.

What counts as clean metal?

Metal only, sorted from your rubbish, with fuel, oil and water drained and gas bottles taken off. Anything that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate.

Is there a fee for anything?

A few items: gas bottles ($15 under 9 kg, $25 over), fire extinguishers ($10 under 3 kg, $15 over), nitrous canisters ($20 each), solar panels ($39 each) and tyres on rims ($25 each). See the gate fees.

Do I need to book?

Not for a car, ute or trailer. We're open Monday to Saturday; call Nigel for gate times. More than a tonne? Get a collection quote instead.

Do you take car batteries?

Yes, and they're no charge. Tape the terminals before you bring them.

Can I bring a whole car?

Call Nigel first. Car bodies and big machinery need a chat before they come in.

Do you collect from homes?

Collections are for a tonne or more, usually from businesses, building sites, farms and councils. If you've got that much at home, ask for a quote. Otherwise, bring it in over a few trips.

What does a collection cost?

One service fee, quoted before we come. It depends on what it is, how much, the work on site, access and distance. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Where do you collect from?

Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria, and further afield by arrangement. Tell us where the site is when you ask for a quote.

Hard drives and data?

We don't take data-bearing drives. Remove or wipe drives and SSDs before we collect servers, computers or cabinets.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, and anything still holding fuel or oil. See the full list.
