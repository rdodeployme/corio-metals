What does a collection cost?

One service fee, quoted before we come. It depends on what it is, how much, the work on site, access and distance. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

No. We don't buy scrap metal. You pay one service fee for the collection, and every load is weighed and docketed.

How soon can you come?

Tell us when suits in the form. Nigel will confirm a day when he sends the quote.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, and anything still holding fuel or oil. See the list.
