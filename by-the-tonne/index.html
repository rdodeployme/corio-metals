    02 · Services

    Four ways we move metal.

    01

    One-off collections

    Strip-outs, refits, clean-ups and site clears. We quote the job, bring the crew and truck, and take it in one go.

    02

    Bins on site

    For steady volumes from workshops, factories and building sites. A bin on site, swapped when it's full.

    03

    Regular collections

    Scheduled pick-ups for depots, multi-site businesses and councils, on a timetable that suits your operation.

    04

    Weighed and reported

    A weighbridge docket for every load, and environmental reporting through YARTA.

    How each service works

    03 · Who we work with

    Business, building, rural and government.

    If a site has a tonne or more of scrap metal, it's our kind of job. These are the places it usually comes from.

    Offices and fit-outs

    Filing cabinets, lockers, shelving, desk frames and server racks, with drives removed or wiped first.

    Warehouses and logistics

    Pallet racking, roll cages, stillages and shelving from moves and refits.

    Builders and demolition

    Structural offcuts, reo, roofing iron, fencing and frames from building and demolition sites.

    Workshops and manufacturing

    Offcuts, old machinery, motors, cable and plant. Often a bin on site, swapped when full.

    Farms and rural properties

    Fencing, star pickets, machinery and dismantled sheds from a clean-up across the property.

    Councils and government

    Depot clean-ups, fencing, signage and old equipment, with a docket for every load.

    04 · On the record

    Every tonne on the record.

    Every load crosses our weighbridge, so you know exactly what left your site. The docket comes to you, and environmental reporting runs through YARTA Environmental Sustainability, a Recycle Group initiative.

    • A weighbridge docket for every load
    • Environmental reporting through YARTA
    • Drives and SSDs removed or wiped before IT collections
    • Batteries removed from equipment travel separately, and we take them too
    How reporting works

    05 · Process

    From first call to docket.

    1. Step 01

      Request

      What it is, roughly how much, where it sits on site and when suits. Photos help.

    2. Step 02

      Quote

      We confirm what we can take and quote one service fee. Nothing is booked until you approve.

    3. Step 03

      Collect

      Our crew and truck, a bin on site or a load on the day. We follow your site rules and inductions.

    4. Step 04

      Weigh

      Every load crosses our weighbridge. The docket comes to you.

    5. Step 05

      Report

      Environmental reporting through YARTA, a Recycle Group initiative.
