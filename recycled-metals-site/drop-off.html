Drop-off · Free, any volume

Bring it to Recycled Metals.

At Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong. Free scrap metal drop-off, any volume, from a bin to a B‑double. Unload under cover, with staff on hand to show where it goes. Semi-trailer and B‑double loads go to Nunjara, Atkinsons Road, Moorabool.

At the gate

Free, with a fee on a few items.

Gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims need extra handling, so they carry a fee. Everything else metal is free.

Recycled Metals doesn't buy scrap metal.

Before you come

Sort it before you come.

Sorted loads are quicker to unload. Staff on site show where each part goes.

  • Metal in one pile, everything else in another
  • Gas bottles off BBQs and heaters
  • Fuel and oil drained from mowers, engines and machinery
  • Mats off trampolines and mattresses off bed frames
  • Car battery terminals taped
  • Enclosed shoes and gloves for unloading

Not accepted

Please leave these at home.

Car bodies and big machinery: call Rory first on 0433 115 500.

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid
