How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

No booking for a car, ute or trailer. Bringing a truck or more than a tonne? Book a collection instead and we'll come to you.

01

Load up

Copper, brass, aluminium and stainless in their own bins or bags, apart from the steel.

02

Drive in

Stay in your vehicle at the gate until the yard team waves you through.

03

Weigh and unload

Weigh in on the weighbridge, unload where you're shown, then weigh out.

04

Get paid

Your metal is graded and paid by bank transfer to your account.

Bring with you

Photo ID and your bank details.

Victorian law has banned cash for scrap metal since May 2018. We have to record your name, address and ID, and any payment goes by bank transfer.

  • Driver licence or other photo ID
  • BSB and account number for payment
  • Enclosed shoes and gloves if you're unloading
  • Kids and pets stay in the vehicle

Get more for your load

Sort it before you come.

A mixed pile is paid at the rate of the lowest metal in it. Ten minutes of sorting at home can be worth more than the trip itself.

Copper

Pipe, cable, hot water cylinders. Bright, clean copper pays the most.

Brass

Taps, valves, fittings and door hardware.

Aluminium

Window frames, sheet, extrusion, rims and cans.

Stainless

Sinks, benches and kitchen equipment.

We can't take

Please leave these at home.

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, paint and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid
  • General rubbish, timber, soil and green waste
  • Metal you can't show is yours to sell

Fridges, freezers, air-cons and car bodies need a call first. Not sure about something? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222 before you load up.
