Fair questions

Ask away.

Still not sure? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

Nope. We don't buy scrap, and clean metal costs nothing to drop off. It's the easy way to clear the shed, and it all goes back into making new metal.

How big is a shedload?

Anything under a tonne: a boot, a ute, a trailer, the whole back shed. Bring it in, no booking needed.

And a truckload?

A tonne or more. We bring the truck, quote one service fee before we come, and weigh every load on our weighbridge.

Anything extra at the gate?

A few things carry a handling fee: gas bottles, fire extinguishers, nitrous canisters, solar panels and tyres on rims. See the gate fees.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, firearms and explosives, clinical waste, and anything still holding fuel or oil.
