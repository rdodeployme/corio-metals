Clean metal

What ‘clean’ means at the gate.

Clean scrap metal is no charge. Anything that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate, so a few minutes at home saves you money.

{{ICON:check}}No charge

  • Metal only, sorted from your rubbish
  • Fuel, oil and water drained
  • Gas bottles off BBQs and heaters
  • Car batteries with the terminals taped

{{ICON:cross}}Sort these out first

  • Timber, plastic, rubber and fabric
  • Mattresses off bed frames
  • Mats, nets and padding off trampolines
  • Tyres off rims, or it's $25 a rim

Rubbish can still come in the same trip. It goes through the transfer station at its usual rate.

Business collections

A tonne or more? We'll come to you.

Businesses, building sites, farms and councils. One service fee, quoted before we come. Our crew does the lifting, and every load gets a weighbridge docket.

1tonne

How much is a tonne? 1,000 kg. About twenty steel filing cabinets, and more than a trailer without brakes is allowed to weigh fully loaded (750 kg).
