Shedload? Drop it off.

Bring it to Furner Avenue.

A boot, a ute, a trailer or the whole back shed. Anything under a tonne comes to Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong. Clean scrap metal is no charge.

How it works

Four steps. No fuss.

Households, trades and small businesses. Sort it at home and you're in and out quick.

  1. 01

    Sort it

    Metal in one pile, rubbish in another. Drain fuel and oil, and take gas bottles off BBQs.

  2. 02

    Load up

    Boot, ute or trailer. Strap it down and cover the load.

  3. 03

    Drive in

    Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue. Open Monday to Saturday; call Nigel for gate times.

  4. 04

    Unload

    Under cover, with staff on hand to point the way. Clean metal is no charge.

At the gate

Most metal costs nothing to leave.

A handful of items carry a handling fee because they need extra work before they can be recycled.

  • Clean scrap metalEvery type of metal, sorted from your rubbishNo charge
  • WhitegoodsFridges, freezers, washers, dryers and ovensNo charge
  • Car batteriesTape the terminalsNo charge
  • Steel bikesWhole or in piecesNo charge
  • Gas bottlesEmpty and customer-owned$15 under 9 kg, $25 over 9 kg
  • Fire extinguishersFully discharged$10 under 3 kg, $15 over 3 kg
  • Nitrous canistersOver 0.5 kg or 2 L$20 each
  • Solar panels$39 each
  • Tyres on rimsRims with the tyre off are scrap metal$25 each

Fees as listed on Recycle North Geelong's price list. Anything in your load that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate. Recycle North Geelong price list

Before you come

Five minutes in the driveway.

  • Gas bottles off BBQs and heaters
  • Fuel and oil drained from mowers, engines and machinery
  • Mats off trampolines and mattresses off bed frames
  • Car battery terminals taped
  • Load strapped down and covered
  • Enclosed shoes and gloves for unloading

Got rubbish too?

Bring it in the same trip.

Recycle North Geelong is a transfer station, so anything that isn't metal goes through at its usual rate. Sort the metal out first and it stays no charge.

Can't take

Leave these at home.

Car bodies and big machinery need a call first. Not sure? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222 before you load up.

  • ×Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • ×Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • ×Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • ×Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • ×Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • ×Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid
