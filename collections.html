Who we collect from

One-off clean-ups or regular loads.

If it's more than a trailer can carry, or it keeps piling up, a collection is the easier way to move it.

Offices and government

Strip-outs and moves: filing cabinets, lockers, compactus, shelving, desk and partition frames, server racks.

Workshops and factories

A bin on site for offcuts, swapped when it's full. Machinery, motors, switchboards, cable and old plant.

Warehouses and logistics

Pallet racking, roll cages, stillages, shelving and pallet jacks when you refit or move.

Building and demolition

Structural steel, reo, roofing, cladding and services stripped out on site. Bins swapped as you go.

Councils and depots

Depot steel, signs, bins, playground and park equipment, and transfer station metal.

Trades and contractors

Plumbers, electricians and fabricators with steady volumes. Regular runs, booked around you.

How a collection works

Quoted first, then gone.

One service fee, quoted before we come. Nothing is booked until you approve it.

01

Book

What it is, roughly how much, where it is on site and when suits. Photos help.

02

Quote

We confirm what we can take and quote one service fee. Big or complex jobs get a site visit first.

03

Collect

A bin drop or loaded on the day. Our crew follows your site rules and inductions.

04

Weigh

Every load crosses our weighbridge at Furner Avenue.

05

Paperwork

A weighbridge docket for every load, with environmental reporting through YARTA.

What sets the quote

Five things move the number.

Every collection carries one service fee, quoted before we come. Nothing is booked until you approve.

What it is

Plain, sorted metal is the quickest to load. Mixed loads and items that need separating take longer.

How much

The weight and volume, and whether it's a one-off load, a bin on site or regular collections.

Work on site

Unbolting racking and benches, dismantling frames and machinery, or cutting steel down to size.

Access and timing

Loading dock or upper floors, stairs and long carries, after-hours work and site inductions.

Distance

Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria. Further afield by arrangement.

We collect

  • Structural steel, plate, pipe and offcuts
  • Pallet racking, shelving and roll cages
  • Filing cabinets, lockers and steel furniture
  • Machinery, motors and plant, drained
  • Copper and aluminium cable, switchboards
  • Stainless benches and kitchen equipment
  • Server racks and cabinets, drives out Check
  • Whitegoods: fridges, washers, dryers, ovens

We can't take

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid
  • Data-bearing drives: remove or wipe them first
