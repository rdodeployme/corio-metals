01 · Public drop-off

Bring it to Furner Avenue.

Anyone can drop off scrap metal at Recycle North Geelong, 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong. Car boot, ute or trailer. Clean metal is no charge, and you unload under cover.

Directions Load check

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

No booking for a car, ute or trailer. Bringing a truck or more than a tonne? Book a collection instead and we'll come to you.

01

Load up

Metal in one pile, everything else in another. Copper, brass and aluminium kept apart if you can.

02

Drive in

Follow the signs at Recycle North Geelong and stay in your vehicle until you're waved through.

03

Unload

Under cover, out of the weather. Staff are on hand to show you which bay it goes in.

04

Drive out

Clean scrap metal is no charge. A few items carry a handling fee, listed below.

Before you come

Five minutes at home saves twenty at the gate.

We don't buy scrap, so there's no haggling over prices. Get the load ready and you're in and out.

  • Gas bottles off BBQs and heaters
  • Fuel and oil drained from mowers, engines and machinery
  • Timber, plastic and fabric stripped off where you can
  • Enclosed shoes and gloves for unloading
  • Kids and pets stay in the vehicle

Gate guide

What it costs at the gate.

Clean scrap metal costs nothing to leave with us. A handful of items carry a handling fee because they need extra work before they can be recycled.

  • Clean scrap metalEvery type of metal, sorted from your rubbishNo charge
  • WhitegoodsFridges, freezers, washers, dryers and ovensNo charge
  • Car batteriesTape the terminalsNo charge
  • Steel bikesWhole or in piecesNo charge
  • Gas bottlesEmpty and customer-owned$15 under 9 kg, $25 over 9 kg
  • Fire extinguishersFully discharged$10 under 3 kg, $15 over 3 kg
  • Nitrous canistersOver 0.5 kg or 2 L$20 each
  • Solar panels$39 each
  • Tyres on rimsRims with the tyre off are scrap metal$25 each

Fees as listed on Recycle North Geelong's price list. Anything in your load that isn't metal goes through the transfer station at its usual rate. Recycle North Geelong price list

Sort it before you come

Every metal has a bay.

Keep these apart from the steel and unloading takes minutes. Mixed piles take longer to sort.

Copper

Pipe, cable, hot water cylinders and motor windings.

Brass

Taps, valves, fittings and door hardware.

Aluminium

Window frames, sheet, extrusion, rims and cans.

Stainless

Sinks, benches and kitchen equipment.

What we takeLoad check

We can't take

Please leave these at home.

  • Asbestos, or anything that might contain it
  • Chemicals, prescribed and hazardous waste
  • Full, charged or hired gas cylinders
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Clinical waste, sharps and radioactive material
  • Anything still holding fuel, oil or liquid

Car bodies and big machinery need a call first. Not sure about something? Call Nigel on 0427 888 222 before you load up.
