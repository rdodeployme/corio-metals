Who it's for

Any business, site or depot with a tonne or more.

One-off clean-outs, strip-outs and site clears, or a bin on site for steady volumes. Tell us what you've got and we'll work out the best way to move it.

Offices and fit-outs

Filing cabinets, lockers, shelving, desk frames and server racks, with drives removed or wiped first.

Warehouses and logistics

Pallet racking, roll cages, stillages and old shelving from moves and refits.

Builders and demolition

Reo, structural offcuts, roofing iron, fencing and frames from building and demolition sites.

Workshops and factories

Offcuts, old machinery, motors, cable and plant. A bin on site, swapped when it's full.

Farms and rural properties

Old fencing, star pickets, machinery and dismantled sheds from a clean-up across the property.

Councils and government

Depot and site clean-ups, fencing, signage and old equipment, with a docket for every load.

The service fee

One fee for the job, quoted first.

We don't buy scrap metal, and you won't get a surprise bill. The service fee depends on the job, and you'll know it before we come.

  • What it is and how muchWeight, volume and how it's sorted.
  • The work on siteWhere it sits, access, stairs and any dismantling.
  • Bin or one-off loadA bin left on site, a single collection, or regular pick-ups.
  • DistanceHow far the site is from North Geelong.

Weighed and reported

A docket for every load.

Every load crosses our weighbridge, so you know exactly what left your site. Environmental reporting through YARTA Environmental Sustainability, a Recycle Group initiative.

  • Weighbridge docketThe weight of every load, sent to you.
  • Environmental reportingThrough YARTA Environmental Sustainability.
  • Data-bearing gearDrives and SSDs removed or wiped before we collect.
